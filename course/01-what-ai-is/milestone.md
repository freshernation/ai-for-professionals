# Module 1 milestone: one real task, done and checked

## What to produce

Complete one real task from your own job with an AI assistant, and write a half-page note about it.

Choose a task you would have done this week anyway: a message, a summary, preparation for a meeting, a first draft of a document. Then:

1. Decide which tier the material belongs to, and strip it down if needed before sharing.
2. Run the whole conversation: brief the assistant, give it the material, steer with at least three follow-ups, and check the result.
3. Use the result for real.

Your note should record:

- The task, and why you judged it suitable for AI (could you recognise a wrong answer, and what would one cost?).
- What you shared, what you held back, and why.
- Your opening prompt.
- One thing the assistant got wrong, invented or missed, and how you caught it.
- Roughly how long it took compared with doing it unaided.

Bring the note to the module session.

## How you know it is good

- The task was real and the result was actually used, not an experiment.
- Nothing from tier 3 went into the assistant, and tier 2 material went only into an approved tool or was stripped down first.
- The conversation had several turns. You steered it; you did not accept the first answer.
- You can point to a specific fact you verified, or a specific invention you removed.
- You can explain, in terms of how the model works, why it got that thing wrong.
