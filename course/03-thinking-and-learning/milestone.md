# Module 3 milestone: learn something you need, and test a decision

## What to produce

Two short pieces of work, both on real matters from your job.

**Part A: a learning plan you have started**

1. Choose something you need to learn in the next two months.
2. Have the assistant interview you and build a plan with a destination, a sequence, an ignore list and weekly milestones. Make sure it tests your baseline with real questions.
3. Choose three or four sources for the first two weeks. Check one of them by leaving it: who is behind it, and how is it regarded?
4. Put the sessions in your calendar.
5. Do week one, including a tutoring session of at least ten minutes in which the assistant questions you.

**Part B: a stress-tested decision**

1. Take a decision or plan you are currently fairly confident about.
2. Run a pre-mortem and ask for the strongest case for the option you rejected.
3. Ask how one other field handles a problem of the same shape.

Bring to the module session:

- Your plan, with the ignore list.
- The baseline question you got wrong, or the gap the tutor found.
- One source you checked, and what you found.
- The most serious objection to your decision, whether it was right, and what you changed.

## How you know it is good

- The destination is stated as something you will be able to do, with a purpose.
- The plan reflects your actual baseline. At least one topic was added or dropped because of how you answered.
- The ignore list has real items on it and the plan fits the time you have.
- Each week ends with something you produce or do. Reading alone does not count.
- At least one source is primary, and you can say who is behind each one.
- In the tutoring session you got something wrong and can explain what the gap was.
- The objection you bring is specific to your plan and checked against a fact you hold. General remarks about risk do not count.
- You changed something in your plan, or you can say exactly why the objection does not apply.
