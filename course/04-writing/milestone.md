# Module 4 milestone: one real piece of writing, drafted, edited and yours

## What to produce

Take one substantial piece of writing you need for work in the next two weeks through the whole process. It can be a report, a proposal, a presentation or an important message. It should matter, and a real person should read or hear it.

1. **Draft.** Start from your own notes or spoken thoughts. Get an outline first, then a draft that uses only what you supplied, with [brackets] for gaps.
2. **Fill the gaps** with real facts.
3. **Edit.** Ask for criticism, with no praise and no rewrite. Sort it into right, wrong and taste. Fix the "right" items yourself.
4. **Check.** For a long document, run the consistency check. For a message, run the tone check. For a presentation, read the titles alone and rehearse the hard questions.
5. **Make it yours.** Read it aloud. Rewrite the opening and the main point in your own words. Remove anything you would not say.
6. **Use it.**

Also build your **style description**. From three or four samples of your own writing, get ten specific observations about how you write, correct them, and save them.

Bring to the module session:

- The first draft and the final version.
- The list of criticisms, marked right, wrong or taste.
- One fact you had to go and find because the draft was vague or bracketed.
- Your style description.

## How you know it is good

- The ideas, conclusions and recommendations are yours. You can say where each came from.
- Every fact, figure and date in the final version has been checked against a source you trust.
- At least one criticism led to a real change, and you rejected at least one for a stated reason.
- Vague phrases have been replaced with specifics or removed.
- In a long document, the summary matches the body and the numbers agree throughout.
- Read aloud, it sounds like you. A colleague who knows you would not find it out of character.
- It contains no commitment you did not intend to make.
- You would be comfortable if the reader knew exactly how it was produced.
