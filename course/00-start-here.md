# Start here

This page takes about ten minutes. It tells you how the course works, shows you where you are starting from, and has you set a goal that is yours, not ours.

## How the course works

There are eight modules of five lessons each. A lesson takes 10 to 15 minutes and always has the same parts:

- **Outcome**: what you will be able to do afterwards.
- **Why it matters**: the work situation it helps with.
- **The idea**: the concept, plainly explained.
- **Worked example**: a realistic task with the full prompt.
- **In your tool**: appears only when ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini behave differently.
- **Try it on your own work**: one exercise using something from your actual job. This is the part that matters most. Reading about AI changes nothing; using it on real work does.
- **Ignore for now**: what you can safely skip at this stage.
- **In the module session**: what the instructor's live session adds.
- **Check yourself**: two or three quick questions.

Each module ends with a **milestone**: one piece of your own work, done with AI, that shows you are ready to move on.

Lessons stand alone. If you need to summarise a 60-page contract tomorrow, go straight to that lesson.

## The live sessions

Each module has one live session with the instructor. The written lessons get you working. The session goes deeper: how things work underneath, harder cases, live demonstrations, and your questions about your own situation. Bring your milestone to the session.

## Where are you starting from?

Answer yes or no. Be honest; nobody sees this but you.

1. I have used an AI assistant at least ten times.
2. I have used one for real work, not only to try it out.
3. I usually give it background about my situation before asking.
4. I have caught an AI answer that was confidently wrong.
5. I know whether my employer allows AI tools, and which ones.
6. I have uploaded a document or spreadsheet and asked questions about it.
7. I have asked an AI to criticise my work, not only to produce work.
8. I have used AI to learn something new, with a plan.
9. I have made something other than text with AI: an image, a chart, a small tool.
10. I have had an AI take an action for me in another app, such as a calendar or inbox.

**0 to 3 yes:** you are starting fresh. Follow the modules in order.
**4 to 7 yes:** you have the basics. Skim module 1, do module 2 properly, then follow your interests.
**8 to 10 yes:** you are already a regular user. Go to the modules where you answered no, and to modules 7 and 8.

## Set your own goal

A course that does not know where you are going can only give you the general tour. Open your assistant and paste the prompt below. It makes the assistant interview you before it advises you, which is a habit you will use throughout the course.

```
I'm a [your role] in [your industry], starting a course on using AI at work.
Before giving me any advice, interview me. Ask one question at a time and
wait for my answer before asking the next.

Find out:
1. What my working week actually consists of.
2. Which tasks take the most time or that I most dislike.
3. What I have already tried with AI, and how it went.
4. What I would like to be able to do in eight weeks that I can't do now.

After about eight questions, give me:
- One sentence stating my goal for the course.
- The three tasks from my week where AI is most likely to help first.
- A short list of AI topics I can ignore for now.
```

Save the answer. You will come back to it at the end of the course, and it will tell you which lessons deserve the most attention.

## What you need

- One assistant: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. A free account is enough to begin.
- Something from your real job to practise on in every lesson.
- A place to keep prompts that worked. A plain notes file is fine.
